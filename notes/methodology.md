# Methodology and provenance

Reviewed 21 November 2026.

## What is being modeled

The intended name is **Benner cycle**, after Samuel Benner. His work examined commodity price patterns. The primary book reviewed here is the **1884 third edition**, not an authenticated 1875 first-edition scan:

https://stock-market-observations.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/benners-prophecies-of-future-ups-and-downs-in-prices-by-samuel-benner.pdf

The book covers pig iron, hogs, corn and provisions. Its historical assertions do not establish forecast validity for modern equities, crypto, Indian markets or property.

## Selected extended-chart dataset

The application uses an explicit, bounded transcription of the extended chart reproduced in this secondary document:

https://www.rocketeller.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Samuel-Benner-1876.pdf

The filename is not proof of publication date or authorship. This reproduction is the selected reference for the starter, not an independently authenticated original. The source and selected dates should be audited against a high-resolution archival chart before quantitative analysis.

| Family | Meaning in the selected chart | Years used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Panic reference | 1927, 1945, 1965, 1981, 1999, 2019, 2035, 2053 |
| B | High-price reference | 1926, 1935, 1945, 1953, 1962, 1972, 1980, 1989, 1999, 2007, 2016, 2026, 2034, 2043, 2053 |
| C | Low-price reference | 1924, 1931, 1942, 1951, 1958, 1968, 1978, 1985, 1996, 2005, 2012, 2023, 2032, 2039, 2050, 2059 |

Some reproductions use 1969 instead of 1968 or other conflicting dates. This project preserves the chosen reproduction rather than silently combining variants. Years beyond the listed bounds are not generated. The app supports 1924–2059 and permits multiple families for the same year (for example, 1999).

## Interpretation rules

- The timeline has three categorical rows, not a price or expected-return axis. Lines simply guide the eye across calendar years.
- Only exact marker years receive a classification. Intervening years have no assigned category; no decline from 2026 to 2032 is presumed.
- A fact is a user-entered observation with a source URL. Supports/challenges/neutral are the user's interpretation, not automatically verified labels.
- Counts represent entered observations, not probability, confidence or independent pieces of evidence. Duplicate or correlated sources can bias counts.
- No live prices, backtest, projected return, buy/sell signal or predictive score is provided.
- To evaluate a hypothesis, specify the asset, measurable outcome, time window, baseline and failure condition before observing the result. Retain misses and contradictory evidence.
